About me

Engineering behind discovery.

I transform heterogeneous computing resources into infrastructure that scientists can use consistently—across local clusters, grids, clouds, leadership-class supercomputers, and emerging computing platforms.

My focus is the software layer between scientific workloads and the computing facilities that execute them. I work on resource provisioning, workload management, authentication, automation, observability, deployment, and the practical integration of HPC systems into high-throughput computing environments.

I also contribute to technical leadership, documentation, mentoring, collaboration, and programs that connect students and researchers with meaningful work in physics, engineering, and computing.
